# 📱 Students' Social Media Addiction Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Short Description

An interactive **Power BI dashboard** that analyzes how social media usage affects students' lives. It explores **705 students** across multiple countries and looks at usage hours, sleep, academic performance, mental health, and relationship conflicts, to show where social media addiction is highest and which groups are most affected.

**Key numbers at a glance**

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Students | 705 |
| Avg Daily Usage Hours | 4.92 |
| Avg Sleep Hours | 6.87 |
| % Affected Academically | 64.26% |
| Avg Addicted Score | 6.44 |

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **Power BI Desktop**: dashboard design and visualization
- **Power Query**: data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX**: measures and calculated fields (averages, % affected, etc.)
- **CSV / Excel**: raw dataset format

---

## 📂 Data Source

- **Dataset:** Students' Social Media Addiction (Kaggle)
- **Size:** 705 student records
- **Main columns:** Student_ID, Age, Gender, Academic_Level, Country, Avg_Daily_Usage_Hours, Most_Used_Platform, Affects_Academic_Performance, Sleep_Hours_Per_Night, Mental_Health_Score, Relationship_Status, Conflicts_Over_Social_Media, Addicted_Score

---

## ✨ Features

- **Executive Overview**: KPI cards (total students, usage, sleep, % affected academically), addiction score by academic level, gender split, and usage trend by age.
- **Academic Impact**: average usage hours by academic level, % affected academically by platform, and an **age range slider** to filter.
- **Mental Health & Sleep**: country-wise rating table, mental health score distribution, and average sleep hours by age, with a **gender slicer**.
- **Relationships & Conflicts**: conflict levels by relationship status, relationship status breakdown, and a student-level table with **Country** and **Academic Level** dropdown filters.
- **Interactive Story View**: toggle between **Gender View** and **Academic Level View**, with platform share shown in a pie chart.
- **Student Profile**: drill-through page with an addiction score gauge and profile KPIs, plus a back button for navigation.
- Consistent dark theme with a gold color palette for easy reading.

### 🔍 Key Insights

- **High School** students have the highest addiction score (**8.0**) and highest usage hours, compared to Undergraduate (6.5) and Graduate (6.2).
- **Instagram** (35.32%), **TikTok** (21.84%) and **Facebook** (17.45%) are the most-used platforms.
- Platforms like **KakaoTalk, WhatsApp, TikTok and Snapchat** show the highest share of students affected academically.
- Average usage is highest at age **18 (5.39 hrs)** and lowest at age **23 (4.51 hrs)**.
- Gender split is almost equal (353 female, 352 male).
- 54.47% of students are Single, 40.99% are In a Relationship, and 4.54% are Complicated.
